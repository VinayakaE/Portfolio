About

Data, models and the software that carries them

I'm a data scientist and backend developer with industry experience at Caliper Business Solutions in Bangalore, where I progressed from data analyst intern to associate data scientist and then associate backend developer.

My work there covered machine learning models, optimization algorithms, MIS reporting, Looker dashboards and REST APIs. I have used the same toolkit to solve practical problems in warehousing, vehicle assignment and real-time tracking.

Since March 2026 I have been studying for an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science for Digital Business Management at Hochschule Furtwangen, following a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering. My Master's projects so far include a freight price prediction pipeline and a computer vision pipeline for ID documents.

I am interested in using data and software to make operations clearer and more efficient, from forecasting cost to planning deliveries to turning raw data into reports people can act on.

Master's Project · HFU · Apr – Jun 2026

FreightIQ

Problem
Predicting freight prices from a large set of shipment records with inconsistent location and distance data.
Solution
A data enrichment pipeline that geocoded locations, resolved destination states, corrected distances and standardized city and state names, followed by a benchmark of five regression models.
Outcome
  • 386K+ shipment records enriched and standardized
  • XGBoost selected as the best of 5 models, compared on MAE, RMSE, R² and MAPE
  • Hyperparameter tuning reduced prediction error
  • Business impact presented in a Looker Studio dashboard
  • LocationIQ
  • Photon
  • OSRM
  • XGBoost
  • Looker Studio

Master's Project · HFU · May – Jun 2026

MIDV-500 Document Pipeline

Problem
Reading text reliably from ID documents captured at different angles and positions.
Solution
An end-to-end pipeline that segments the document with a U-Net (ResNet34 backbone), applies perspective rectification to straighten it, and extracts text with PaddleOCR.
Outcome
  • Full pipeline run on the complete MIDV-500 dataset
  • U-Net
  • ResNet34
  • PaddleOCR
  • Computer vision
